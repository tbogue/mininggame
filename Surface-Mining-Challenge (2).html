Explore. Extract. Restore.

Build a mining company.
Leave a living landscape.

Find hidden copper deposits, move overburden, process ore, and invest in real mining equipment. Every mineral has a cost. Can your team earn a profit while protecting the land and water?

Set up your class

Each company receives the same hidden geology and $5,000. Rotate teams after each complete round. No fast clicking required; actions are limited by equipment and budgets.

Give everyone a job

Field geologist

Choose where to survey. Compare grade and depth.

Mine operator

Select columns to excavate. Plan around overburden and safe slopes.

Processing operator

Process one richest block or run the plant up to capacity. Budget for the resulting waste.

Accountant

Track revenue, costs, profit, and upgrade returns.

Environmental engineer

Manage tailings, pollution, and land reclamation.

Smaller teams can combine roles. Team competition is an addition to the source activity. The goal still includes a healthy environment.

What changes in nature?

Clearing vegetation destroys habitat. Exposed soil erodes; excavation creates dust. Processing uses energy and leaves tailings. Backfilling and revegetation begin recovery, but a mature habitat takes years to return. Pollution cleanup reduces current burden; it cannot erase every past impact.
